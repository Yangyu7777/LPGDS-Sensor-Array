# Supporting Information — C18:2 near the WT L-PGDS–DPH endpoint (FA_R2)

## Scope and starting structure

This archive documents the FA_R2 branch: one WT L-PGDS, one neutral all-trans DPH, and one (9E,12E)-octadeca-9,12-dienoate monoanion (CAS 506-21-8; residue FAM). It does not contain PFAS. The protein and DPH starting coordinates were taken from the full-precision final state of the deposited WT–DPH R3 trajectory at parent time 5502 ps (`../DPH_LPGDS_WT_MD/raw/latest-state.xml`, after restoration). The SHA-256 of that source state is d68f371439b7337671cb7c0611c32838d98d32b4956095bdeb1e5007802060ce. Retained protein/DPH coordinate differences after insertion were 0 Å. Authoritative branch starting coordinates are `inputs/coordinates_nm.npy`; the PDB is rounded for storage.

The parent protein has 176 residues including a C-terminal His6 tag (171–176). Its Cys68–Cys165 disulfide and fixed protonation assignments were retained, including neutral HID His90 and tag histidines. The parent model had been checked using PDBFixer and hydrogens regenerated at nominal pH 7.4; no new protein remodeling was performed for this branch. Original protein accession and original DPH-placement algorithm remain incompletely documented; see the parent protocol rather than inferring missing provenance.

The fatty acid was added outside the protein heavy-atom convex hull near the resident DPH. A local geometric search selected a nonoverlapping orientation (placement cone tilt 15°, azimuth 60°, twist 60°; see `provenance/preparation.json`). Initial minimum fatty-acid–WT and fatty-acid–DPH heavy-atom separations were 6.097 and 8.160 Å, respectively. Twenty-four clashing waters were removed and another water was replaced with one Na+ to neutralize the added −1 charge. This selected local exterior position is not an independently established natural entrance or a randomized bulk-solvent start. The deposited FAM SDF/JSON and serialized system record its geometry and charges; no unrecorded docking or optimization method is inferred. FAM is the E/E isomer, not ordinary Z/Z linoleate.

## Force fields and composition

Amber ff14SB described the protein and TIP3P described water/monatomic ions. Small molecules used OpenFF Sage 2.2.1 with stored AM1-BCC charges; the preparation environment used OpenFF Toolkit 0.19.0, openmmforcefields 0.16.0, and AmberTools 23.3. DPH, DMSO and phosphate parameters were inherited from the parent setup; the FAM parameter record and small-molecule cache are deposited in `inputs/`. Mixture thermodynamics and binding energetics were not independently validated.

The initial periodic cube measured 82.451399 Å per side. The neutral system comprised 55,359 atoms: one protein, one DPH, one C18:2 anion, 16,729 waters, 224 DMSO molecules, one H2PO4−, three HPO4²−, 53 Na+, two K+, and 49 Cl−. PBS-like ions and nominal 5% v/v DMSO were inherited from the parent's finite-composition model. No repacking to exact experimental concentrations was performed. At the starting volume, each solute species is approximately 2.96 mM; the simulation is 1:1:1 and does not represent the experimental 8 μM/1 μM/10 μM or 8:1:10 ratio. Nominal pH 7.4 denotes fixed protonation, not constant-pH MD or dynamic buffering.

## MD integration and staging

Python 3.12.15 and OpenMM 8.6.1 were used on Apple M5 Pro OpenCL device 0 in single precision. Periodic PME electrostatics used a 1.0 nm cutoff and Ewald tolerance 0.0005. Lennard-Jones interactions used a 1.0 nm cutoff, no switching function, and analytical dispersion correction. Bonds involving hydrogen were constrained and waters rigid; hydrogen mass repartitioning was not applied. The LangevinMiddle integrator used a 1 fs step, 1 ps−1 friction, and seed 2026102102. The 1 bar Monte Carlo barostat operated at 298.15 K every 25 steps during NPT, with seed 2026102153.

Starting velocities were reassigned, so this is not a checkpoint continuation of the parent trajectory. Energy minimization used tolerance 10 kJ mol−1 nm−1 and at most 1000 iterations. Solute heavy atoms (WT, DPH and FAM) were position-restrained using 0.5 k d² with periodic distances during early preparation. A 2 ps 50 K NVT safety test with k=1000 kJ mol−1 nm−2 was followed by 20 ps heating from 50 to 298.15 K and 80 ps NVT at k=1000; 100 ps NPT at k=1000; 100 ps NPT at k=100; and 200 ps unrestrained NPT. Heating updated the target temperature every 5 ps. Production then ran for 5000 ps unrestrained NPT at 298.15 K and 1 bar. No directed pulling, ligand-entry bias, or DPH-expulsion force was applied during production.

Total branch time was 5502 ps. Solute coordinates and thermodynamic records were saved every 1 ps beginning at 3 ps (5500 solute frames including preparation); full coordinates were saved every 10 ps (550 full frames). The analyzed production interval is total time 503–5502 ps: 5000 solute frames. Production time is defined as (total time−502 ps)/1000 ns. The production-average temperature and density were 298.237 K and 1.01327 g mL−1, respectively. These checks do not establish equilibrium convergence.

## Analysis definitions

Periodic minimum-image heavy-atom distances defined pairwise DPH–WT, FAM–WT, and DPH–FAM proximity; contact required a minimum separation <4.5 Å. Residue contact fractions are the fraction of production frames in which any ligand heavy atom contacts any heavy atom of the specified residue. Heatmaps display 50 ps windows, with each pixel showing contact occupancy across 50 saved frames. These contacts are not specifically hydrogen bonds and occupancy is not an equilibrium binding probability.

Protein core RMSD uses Kabsch fitting of the first 170 protein Cα atoms to the branch pre-MD configuration (the parent endpoint). Protein Cα RMSF is calculated relative to the production mean after that fitting. The His6 tag remains in the structure and RMSF output but is excluded from the alignment core. Rg is mass-weighted over protein heavy atoms. Ligand RMSDs are reported after protein fitting and after ligand self-fitting; centroid displacement is relative to the branch initial coordinates in the protein-fitted frame. The fixed outward projection is a geometric descriptor inherited from the source preparation, not a validated pocket reaction coordinate. All input residue numbering is retained.

DPH SASA uses a sphere-point method with a 1.4 Å probe, 960 points per atom including hydrogens, and element radii H 1.2, C 1.7, N 1.55, O 1.52, S 1.8 Å. Three calculations use identical ligand conformations: isolated DPH, DPH occluded by WT only, and DPH occluded by WT+FAM. Occluded fraction is 1−SASA(environment)/SASA(isolated). SASA was evaluated every 50 ps at total times 550–5500 ps (100 frames); its last point is 2 ps before the trajectory endpoint. The difference between WT-only and WT+FAM fractions quantifies additional geometric occlusion by FAM, without changing protein/ligand conformations.

Full-system frames at total times 510–5500 ps (500 frames) were used to count water oxygen atoms within 5 Å of any DPH heavy atom and distinct DMSO molecules with any heavy atom within 4.5 Å of DPH. These are neighbor counts, not hydrogen bonds or solvent dielectric constants. Different observables have distinct sampling intervals and denominators. NumPy/SciPy/Matplotlib are used for analysis; exact installed versions and dependency details accompany the scripts. All plots use English labels and no background grids.

## Interpretation and replicate context

DPH–WT, FAM–WT, and DPH–FAM contact fractions were 1.0000, 0.9962 and 0.9460. Mean protein-only and WT+FAM DPH occlusion were 0.65530 and 0.76295. Mean protein core RMSD was 1.363 Å and protein heavy-atom Rg 16.276 Å. These describe one outcome selected after examining the simulations. FA/control summaries of three velocity repeats are retained in `analysis/FA_control_repeat_context.csv`; their raw trajectories are outside the present module. Identical replica-number seeds do not make different-composition branches bitwise paired trajectories.

FA_R1 behaved differently from FA_R2/R3. Temporal frames are correlated and must not be treated as independent experimental replicates. Late protein RMSD/Rg changes preclude claiming convergence. The fatty acid was already near DPH at the beginning of production after preparation. This dataset does not alone establish enclosed-cavity entry, cooperative affinity, replacement, fluorescence enhancement, or a free-energy landscape. The parent and branch were separately equilibrated and their production time axes must not be joined as a seamless 10 ns trajectory.

## Reproduction and data integrity

`inputs/system.xml` and `raw/running-system.xml` are authoritative serialized potentials. Complete trajectories, stage endpoints, states and checkpoint are losslessly gzip-segmented where needed. `scripts/restore_data.py` verifies parts and reconstructed originals, and `scripts/verify_data.py` checks `DATA_SHA256.csv`. `scripts/analyze_FA_R2.py` recalculates raw observables and `scripts/plot_FA_R2.py` regenerates displayed figures. `scripts/run_FA_R2.py` is an optional OpenCL rerun using the stored inputs; it writes `reproduced_FA_R2/` and does not overwrite archived data. RDKit is required only for the optional MD runner's E/Z checks. A binary checkpoint may require matching hardware/software; the serialized state/system and full-precision coordinates provide portable records. Numerical reruns need not reproduce identical trajectories.
