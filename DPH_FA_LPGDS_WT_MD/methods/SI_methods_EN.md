# Supporting Information methods: WT–DPH with cis,cis-linoleate

A cis,cis-linoleate branch was constructed from the full-precision final coordinates of the selected WT L-PGDS–DPH trajectory (parent time 5502 ps). The protein comprised 176 residues including a retained C-terminal His6 tag and Cys68–Cys165 disulfide. The parent's fixed protonation states at nominal pH 7.4 were retained. In the parent preparation, PDBFixer 1.12.0 found no missing residues or heavy atoms; existing hydrogen records were removed and hydrogens regenerated with OpenMM Modeller. His90 and the six tag histidines were neutral HID; the N terminus was protonated and the C terminus retained OXT. Neutral all-trans DPH (C18H16) and the protein were inherited without new remodeling. Protein/DPH coordinates after fatty-acid insertion differed from the source by 0 Å. This trajectory is a newly equilibrated, reassigned-velocity branch, not a continuous checkpoint extension.

The experimental fatty acid was identified as (9Z,12Z)-octadeca-9,12-dienoic acid (CAS 60-33-3; PubChem CID 5280450), simulated as its −1 linoleate anion (FAM; C18H31O2−). A stereospecified molecule was generated with RDKit ETKDGv3 (seed 20261022) and MMFF geometry optimization (maximum 5000 iterations; converged). Double-bond positions and Z stereochemistry were explicitly checked; prepared carbon-chain double-bond dihedrals were approximately −0.11° and −2.46°. AM1-BCC charges were independently generated using AmberTools rather than copied from the previous E/E isomer. The prior CAS 506-21-8 E/E calculation does not represent this reagent.

Linoleate was positioned outside the WT heavy-atom convex hull near the resident DPH by the same exterior-clearance search as the preceding branch design. Initial minimum linoleate–WT and linoleate–DPH distances were 6.559 and 6.133 Å. Twenty-three overlapping waters were removed and one further water replaced by Na+ to neutralize the added anion. This targeted local start does not represent association from randomized bulk solvent. The neutral model contained 55,362 atoms: one protein, one DPH, one linoleate, 16,730 waters, 224 DMSO molecules, one H2PO4−, three HPO4²−, 53 Na+, two K+, and 49 Cl−, in an initial 8.24514 nm cubic cell. The inherited model approximated PBS with nominal 5% v/v DMSO; its 1:1:1 millimolar effective solute concentrations differ from the experimental micromolar conditions and 8:1:10 ratio.

Amber ff14SB, TIP3P, and OpenFF Sage 2.2.1 described the protein, water/monatomic ions, and small molecules, respectively. Stored AM1-BCC charges and serialized system XML record the actual force-field assignment; mixture thermodynamics were not independently validated. OpenMM 8.6.1 with Python 3.12.15 ran on Apple M5 Pro OpenCL in single precision. PME electrostatics used a 1.0 nm cutoff and 0.0005 Ewald tolerance; Lennard-Jones used a 1.0 nm cutoff, no switching function and dispersion correction. Bonds involving hydrogen were constrained, water was rigid, and hydrogen mass repartitioning was not used. LangevinMiddle integration used a 1 fs step and 1 ps−1 friction. The original run ID was FA_R2, velocity seed 2026102212, and barostat seed 2026102263 (1 bar, 298.15 K, 25-step attempts during NPT).

Minimization (10 kJ mol−1 nm−1 tolerance, maximum 1000 iterations) was followed by a 2 ps safety test at 50 K. Preparation comprised 20 ps heating to 298.15 K, 80 ps NVT, 100 ps strongly restrained NPT, 100 ps weakly restrained NPT and 200 ps unrestrained NPT. Position restraints acted on solute heavy atoms, including both ligands, with force constants 1000 or 100 kJ mol−1 nm−2 during restrained stages. Five ns of unrestrained NPT production followed at 298.15 K and 1 bar. DPH trans and linoleate cis double-bond geometries were monitored separately; no directed ligand-entry or expulsion bias was applied. Three velocity repeats were predefined; this package presents a user-requested trajectory from the new batch, while available repeat/control summaries are retained. Solute coordinates were saved every 1 ps and complete coordinates every 10 ps. Production comprised 5000 solute frames at total times 503–5502 ps; mean temperature and density were 298.243 K and 1.01320 g mL−1.

Protein fitting used 170 core Cα atoms and branch pre-MD coordinates as reference. RMSF was calculated relative to the aligned production mean; Rg was mass-weighted over protein heavy atoms. Protein-fitted and ligand-self-fitted RMSDs distinguished relative motion from internal deformation. Pairwise and residue contacts required any relevant heavy-atom periodic distance <4.5 Å. Contact heatmaps used 50 ps bins (50 saved frames per bin). Simultaneous three-pair contact required DPH–WT, linoleate–WT and DPH–linoleate criteria in the same frame; contiguous episodes were estimated at the 1 ps saving interval. Contact cutoffs of 3.5, 4.0, 4.5 and 5.0 Å were tabulated for sensitivity analysis.

DPH SASA was evaluated with a 1.4 Å probe and 960 sphere points per atom, including hydrogen, at 100 production frames separated by 50 ps. Identical DPH conformations were evaluated alone, with WT occlusion, and with WT+linoleate occlusion; occluded fraction was 1−SASA(environment)/SASA(isolated). Water oxygen atoms within 5 Å of DPH heavy atoms and distinct DMSO molecules with a heavy atom within 4.5 Å were counted in 500 full-system production frames. The last SASA/full-solvent sample is at total 5500 ps, 2 ps before the endpoint. Means of differently sampled observables use their own denominators. Numerical descriptors are geometric, not hydrogen-bond definitions or direct fluorescence observables.

PyMOL coordinate renders used a common camera for the four chronological snapshots; the His6 tag was hidden only for visualization. A separately labeled contact illustration used the midpoint of the longest simultaneous-contact episode (production 3.890 ns); a close-up uses a different zoom and semi-transparent protein. No atomic coordinates were manually moved for rendering. This selected illustration does not establish enclosed-cavity residence. All trajectory statistics are descriptive and correlated; no frame-as-replicate significance testing, equilibrium affinity, stable ternary occupancy or fluorescence enhancement is claimed. Original protein accession and parent DPH-placement metadata remain incomplete and require primary-record confirmation before submission.

The DPH–WT residue-contact panel is an internal observable of the ternary trajectory, not a separately simulated binary control. Original parent R3 data are provided separately as a reference. No-fatty-acid CONTROL_R1–R3 are re-equilibrated endpoint-continuation branches and use a different RMSD reference from the original parent trajectory.
