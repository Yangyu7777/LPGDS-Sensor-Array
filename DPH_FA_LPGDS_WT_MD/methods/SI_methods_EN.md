# Molecular dynamics simulations

## Supporting Information methods

An all-atom MD calculation was performed to examine the behavior of C18:2 near an existing WT L-PGDS–DPH configuration. The initial protein and DPH coordinates were taken without modification from the final full-precision state of the deposited WT–DPH R3 calculation (parent time 5502 ps). The protein comprised 176 residues including a C-terminal His6 tag and retained the Cys68–Cys165 disulfide and the parent's fixed protonation assignments at nominal pH 7.4. One (9E,12E)-octadeca-9,12-dienoate anion (CAS 506-21-8; FAM) was placed outside the protein near DPH, with initial minimum heavy-atom separations of 6.10 Å from WT and 8.16 Å from DPH. Clashing waters were removed and one Na+ was added for charge neutrality. The protein structural accession and original parent DPH-placement metadata require confirmation from primary records.

Amber ff14SB was used for protein, TIP3P for water and monatomic ions, and OpenFF Sage 2.2.1 with stored AM1-BCC charges for the small molecules. The inherited PBS/DMSO-containing solvent model comprised 16,729 waters, 224 DMSO molecules, one H2PO4−, three HPO4²−, 53 Na+, two K+, and 49 Cl−. The neutral system contained 55,359 atoms in an initial cubic cell of 8.24514 nm per side. This 1:1:1 model has millimolar effective solute concentrations; it does not reproduce the experimental micromolar concentrations or 8:1:10 ratio. Nominal 5% v/v DMSO and PBS-like composition describe the finite simulation model, whose mixture properties were not independently validated.

OpenMM 8.6.1 was run on Apple M5 Pro OpenCL in single precision using periodic boundary conditions, PME electrostatics, a 1.0 nm cutoff, hydrogen-bond constraints, rigid water, and a 1 fs LangevinMiddle integration step (friction 1 ps−1; seed 2026102102). Following minimization and a 2 ps test at 50 K, the system underwent 20 ps heating to 298.15 K, 80 ps restrained NVT, 100 ps strongly restrained NPT, 100 ps weakly restrained NPT, and 200 ps unrestrained NPT. Solute heavy-atom restraint constants were 1000 or 100 kJ mol−1 nm−2. A 5 ns unrestrained NPT trajectory was then obtained at 298.15 K and 1 bar, with coordinates saved every 1 ps for solute and every 10 ps for the complete system. Velocities were reassigned, so this branch is not a continuous checkpoint extension. No directed entry or displacement force was applied in production.

Protein fitting used 170 core Cα atoms and the branch initial coordinates as reference. RMSD, RMSF, mass-weighted protein heavy-atom Rg, ligand motion, and pairwise periodic heavy-atom contacts (<4.5 Å) were evaluated. Residue-contact heatmaps report occupancy in 50 ps windows. DPH SASA was calculated with a 1.4 Å probe and 960 sphere points per atom, at 100 production frames, considering isolated DPH, WT occlusion, and WT+C18:2 occlusion at identical conformations. Water oxygen neighbors within 5 Å and distinct DMSO molecules within 4.5 Å of DPH were counted using 500 full-system production frames. These descriptors characterize proximity and geometric shielding, not hydrogen bonds, affinity, fluorescence changes, or enclosed-pocket residence. This trajectory was selected after reviewing outcomes; contextual FA/control repeat summaries are deposited, and correlated frames are not treated as independent replicates. Detailed staging, analysis definitions, and complete restorable data are provided in the repository.
