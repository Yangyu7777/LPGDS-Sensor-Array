# Molecular dynamics simulations

## Supporting Information methods

All-atom molecular dynamics (MD) simulations were performed to examine the structural behavior of 1,6-diphenyl-1,3,5-hexatriene (DPH) in the presence of wild-type lipocalin-type prostaglandin D synthase (WT L-PGDS). The prepared protein model comprised 176 residues, including a C-terminal His6 tag. Protein coordinates were inspected with PDBFixer 1.12.0 and hydrogen atoms were generated using OpenMM Modeller at nominal pH 7.4. The Cys68–Cys165 disulfide bridge was retained. Neutral all-trans DPH was geometry-refined with RDKit 2023.9.6, and a locally specified near-protein exterior configuration was used as the initial complex, with an initial minimum protein–DPH heavy-atom separation of approximately 4.03 Å. The original structure accession and ligand-placement protocol require confirmation from the primary preparation records.

The protein was modeled with Amber ff14SB, and water/monatomic ions with TIP3P. DPH, dimethyl sulfoxide (DMSO), and phosphate were assigned OpenFF Sage 2.2.1 parameters, with AM1-BCC charges generated through AmberTools 23.3. The cubic periodic box initially measured 8.2116 nm per side and contained one protein, one DPH molecule, 16,754 water molecules, 224 DMSO molecules, one H2PO4−, three HPO4²−, 52 Na+, two K+, and 49 Cl− ions (55,382 atoms). The model was constructed to approximate a PBS/DMSO-containing environment; its discrete initial composition corresponded to approximately 138 mM added NaCl, 3 mM added KCl, 12 mM total phosphate, and nominal 5% (v/v) DMSO. The effective protein and DPH concentrations were each approximately 3 mM and were not intended to reproduce the experimental micromolar concentrations.

Simulations were run with OpenMM 8.6.1 using periodic boundary conditions, particle mesh Ewald electrostatics, a 1.0 nm nonbonded cutoff, and a 1 fs integration step. A LangevinMiddleIntegrator was used with a collision frequency of 1 ps−1. After energy minimization (maximum 1,000 iterations), a 2 ps test at 50 K was followed by 500 ps of preparation: 20 ps heating to 298.15 K, 80 ps restrained NVT, 100 ps strongly restrained NPT, 100 ps weakly restrained NPT, and 200 ps unrestrained NPT. Solute heavy-atom restraint constants during the restrained stages were 1,000 or 100 kJ mol−1 nm−2. A 5 ns unrestrained NPT production simulation was then performed at 298.15 K and 1 bar. Coordinates were saved every 1 ps for the solute and every 10 ps for the complete solvated system.

The trajectory was aligned on the 170 core protein Cα atoms to calculate RMSD and RMSF. Protein–DPH contacts were defined as any intermolecular heavy-atom distance below 4.5 Å, using periodic minimum-image distances. Solvent-accessible surface area (SASA) was evaluated with a 1.4 Å probe, and the ligand surface occluded by the protein was expressed as 1 − SASA(complex)/SASA(isolated). Water and DMSO neighbors were quantified from the full-system trajectory. These descriptors characterize the deposited trajectory and do not independently establish equilibrium affinity, spontaneous binding from bulk solution, or residence within an enclosed binding pocket. Detailed parameters, outputs, and scripts are provided in the public repository.
